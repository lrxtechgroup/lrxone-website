Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

LRX Tech Group (Pty) Ltd ("LRX Tech Group", "we", "us") operates LRX One Core (app.lrxone.com), a business-to-business enterprise operating system. This policy explains what personal information we collect through LRX One Core, how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it, in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA").

1.Who we are

LRX One Core is a B2B platform — our direct customers are businesses ("tenants"), and the personal information we process belongs to those businesses' own employees and authorised users. LRX One Core is not directed at, and does not knowingly collect information from, individual consumers or minors.

2.Information we collect

We collect the following categories of personal information through LRX One Core:

  • Account and identity data — name, work email address, job title, and authentication credentials for each user your organisation invites to the platform.
  • Usage data — actions taken within the platform (workflows created, documents uploaded, features used) needed to operate and improve the service.
  • Content you provide — documents, workflow configurations, and other business data your organisation stores or processes through LRX One Core.
  • Communications with the AI Assistant — messages you send to LRX One Core's AI features, processed to generate responses.
  • Billing information — processed on our behalf by our payment processors (see Section 4); we do not store full card numbers ourselves.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, and device information, collected automatically for security and service-reliability purposes.

3.How we use it

We process personal information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and secure the LRX One Core platform for your organisation.
  • Authenticate users and enforce your organisation's access controls.
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate service-related notices.
  • Improve platform features, informed by aggregated usage patterns.
  • Comply with our own legal obligations, including South African financial recordkeeping requirements.

4.Who we share it with

We share personal information only with third-party service providers ("operators", in POPIA terms) who process it on our behalf to deliver LRX One Core, under contractual confidentiality and security obligations. We do not sell personal information.

5.Data retention

  • Account and platform data is retained for 12 months after subscription cancellation, after which it is deleted unless you request earlier deletion (see Section 7).
  • Financial records (invoices, payment records) are retained for 7 years, as required under South African tax and financial legislation (FICA).

6.Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the information we hold, including encryption in transit, access controls scoped to each tenant, and infrastructure hosted in a secured AWS environment. No system is completely immune to risk, and we continuously work to improve our safeguards.

7.Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to our legal retention obligations (Section 5).
  • Object to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interest.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

If you're an individual user of a tenant on LRX One Core, requests about your own account data should generally go through your organisation's administrator first, since they control your account. Tenant administrators can submit a deletion request for their organisation's data directly through the platform's tenant deletion-request endpoint, or by contacting our Information Officer at brandon@lrxtechgroup.com.

8.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to active tenants by email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.

9.Contact us

Questions about this policy or how we handle personal information can be directed to our Information Officer, Brandon Le Roux, at brandon@lrxtechgroup.com.
